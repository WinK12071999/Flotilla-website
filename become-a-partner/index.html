Become a Partner

Partnership is at the heart of the Flotilla for Friendship.

We welcome Indigenous organizations, community groups, public agencies, first responder services and other organizations that share our commitment to youth, community connection and reconciliation.

How Partners Can Contribute

01

Supporting cultural, educational or recreational activities

02

03

04

05

Contributing expertise, resources or services

06

Supporting outreach and community engagement

Partnership opportunities can be tailored to your organization’s capacity and area of expertise.
