Youth Leadership

Youth Involvement

The Flotilla for Friendship is about more than one day.

We want Indigenous youth to have meaningful opportunities to participate, lead, build connections and help shape the future of the event.

Youth Can Get Involved By

01

Joining the Youth Leadership Working Group

02

Participating in the annual Flotilla for Friendship

03

Sharing ideas and feedback

04

Supporting activities and community engagement

05

Connecting with Indigenous organizations, community partners and first responders

06

Exploring future leadership and volunteer opportunities

Join the 2027 Youth Leadership Working Group

Applications are now open for Indigenous youth and young adults interested in helping shape the 2027 Flotilla for Friendship.

Interested youth can apply by completing the 2027 Indigenous Youth Leadership Working Group application form.

Application deadline: October 16, 2026 at 5:00 p.m.
