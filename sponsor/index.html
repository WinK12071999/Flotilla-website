Become a Sponsor

Support the Flotilla for Friendship and help create meaningful opportunities for Indigenous youth, first responders and community members to connect through culture, shared experiences and relationship building.

Your support helps make Flotilla possible by contributing to programming, meals, activities, transportation, equipment and the overall participant experience.

Sponsorship Levels

Presenting Sponsor | $10,000

Our highest level of sponsorship, offering prominent recognition throughout the event.

  • Recognition as Presenting Sponsor
  • Prominent logo placement on event materials and signage
  • Recognition during opening and closing ceremonies
  • Opportunity to provide welcome remarks
  • Premium exhibitor space
  • Recognition on social media and promotional materials
  • Recognition in the post event report
  • Opportunity to include promotional items in participant packages

Gold Sponsor | $5,000

  • Large logo placement on event materials
  • Recognition during the opening ceremony
  • Exhibitor space
  • Recognition on social media
  • Recognition in the post event report

Silver Sponsor | $2,500

  • Logo placement on event signage
  • Recognition on social media
  • Exhibitor space
  • Recognition in the post event report

Bronze Sponsor | $1,000

  • Logo on sponsor recognition signage
  • Recognition on social media
  • Recognition in the post event report

Community Sponsor | Under $1,000

Every contribution makes a difference. We also welcome financial and in kind support, including:

  • Food and beverages
  • Event supplies
  • Promotional items
  • Transportation support
  • Volunteer support
  • Cultural activity materials
  • Prizes and giveaways

Support the Flotilla

Whether through financial sponsorship, in kind support or community collaboration, your contribution helps strengthen connection and create a meaningful experience for participants.
