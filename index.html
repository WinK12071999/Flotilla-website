25th Anniversary Edition

What to Expect at Flotilla 2026

Founded 25 years ago by the late Algonquin leader Lynda Kitchikeesic, the Flotilla offers Indigenous youth and first responders a safe, supportive environment to connect outside formal settings.

01

Paddling & Journey

Canoeing along the historic Rideau waterways, learning teamwork and relying on each other in the same canoe.

  • Mooney’s Bay Beach launch
  • Paddle to Dow’s Lake
  • Return paddle and closing at Mooney’s Bay
02

Culture & Connection

Honouring Indigenous traditions with Elder blessings, cultural activities, and open dialogue between youth and officers.

  • Cultural activities & knowledge sharing
  • Shared community lunch provided
  • Interactive team-building games
03

Reconciliation in Action

Creating opportunities for Indigenous youth and first responders to build trust, understanding and meaningful relationships through shared experiences.

  • Indigenous youth of all ages
  • Police, first responders and community partners
  • Building relationships beyond the uniform
