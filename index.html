25th Anniversary Edition

What to Expect at Flotilla 2026

Founded 25 years ago by the late Algonquin leader Lynda Kitchikeesic, the Flotilla offers Indigenous youth and first responders a safe, supportive environment to connect outside formal settings.

01

Paddling & Journey

Canoeing along the historic Rideau waterways, learning teamwork and relying on each other in the same canoe.

  • Black Rapids Lock Station launch
  • Stop at Mooney's Bay for lunch & games
  • Closing circle at Dow's Lake Pavilion
02

Culture & Connection

Honouring Indigenous traditions with Elder blessings, cultural activities, and open dialogue between youth and officers.

  • Cultural activities & knowledge sharing
  • Shared community lunch provided
  • Interactive team-building games
03

Reconciliation in Action

Breaking down barriers and fostering trust through authentic human connection on the water and land.

  • Indigenous youth ages 10–18
  • Police & emergency responder volunteers
  • 25-year legacy of healing & respect

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers for youth, guardians, officers, and community volunteers.

The event is open to Indigenous youth ages 10–18 through community partner organizations (Wabano, Odawa, Minwaashin, Inuuqatigiit), along with police officers and first responder volunteers from Ottawa Police Service, OPP, RCMP, Sûreté du Québec, and regional emergency services.
No prior canoeing experience is necessary! Experienced paddlers and facilitators accompany every boat. Safety marine teams escort the flotilla, and approved personal flotation devices (lifejackets) are provided and mandatory for all participants.
Canoes, paddles, lifejackets, a fully catered community lunch at Mooney's Bay, cultural activities, games, water stations, and youth support facilitators are all provided free of charge.
Registration opens June 1 / July 8 and strictly closes on Saturday, August 8, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. EDT to allow final logistcs, meal planning, and boat assignments.
